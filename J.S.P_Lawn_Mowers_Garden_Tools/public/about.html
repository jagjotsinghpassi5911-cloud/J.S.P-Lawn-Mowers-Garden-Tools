J.S.P LAWN MOWERS & GARDEN TOOLS

Tools are just the beginning.

Founded in 1990 with the backing of the late S. Jagdish Singh Passi, J.S.P is a Ludhiana-based manufacturer of lawn mowers and garden tools.

The collection spans power mowers, secateurs, shears, khurpas, watering accessories and other garden essentials. J.S.P also welcomes enquiries about tools made to a customer’s specifications.

A LITTLE HELP
Before you
 get growing.
How do I find the right mower?
Consider your lawn size, the terrain and how often you mow. Share these details and your preferred models with J.S.P to confirm a suitable configuration.
Where can I see prices and availability?
Contact J.S.P with the model numbers in your enquiry list. This catalogue does not display unverified prices or live stock levels.
Can I ask about a custom tool?
Yes. The official J.S.P website invites enquiries about products made to customer specifications. Use “Custom requirements” in the enquiry form.
What happens to my saved enquiry list?
It stays in this browser. You can download it or include it in an email draft. It is not synced across devices.
